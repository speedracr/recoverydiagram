# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: dependency-free static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the smallest portable presentation artifact.

## Users

The artifact is for an interview or screen-sharing audience assessing the author's understanding of German Legal Recovery operations.

## Product Purpose

Explain how heterogeneous claims move through a largely deterministic legal process, including client-specific approvals, court feedback, payments, disputes, title creation, and enforcement decisions.

## Positioning

This is a presentation artifact, not a product prototype or workflow editor. Its distinctive mechanism is a stable legal-process map animated by twenty deterministic synthetic claims.

## Operating Context

Viewed in a full desktop browser or during screen sharing, primarily at laptop and presentation-screen widths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One wide process flow with deterministic replay and play/pause control.
- Twenty fixed synthetic cases across four fictitious clients.
- No backend, persistence, authentication, real data, analytics, or editing.
- The reference diagram is the process source of truth.
- Narrow screens scroll horizontally instead of reflowing the legal process.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference diagram: `/Users/jonas/Downloads/shapes at 26-09-21 22.30.28.png`
- Detailed written process and scenario brief supplied with the request.
- All case data is illustrative and must remain clearly labelled as synthetic.

## Product Principles

- Keep the legal process stable and let moving claims tell the story.
- Preserve legal and operational distinctions without modelling every exception.
- Make heterogeneous client behaviour visible without randomness.
- Prefer presentation clarity over application chrome or framework architecture.
